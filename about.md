# <c-FF6B6B>H</c><c-FF7F50>a</c><c-FFD93D>r</c><c-6BCB77>m</c><c-4D96FF>o</c><c-9B72CF>n</c><c-FF6B6B>y</c>

<cy>Harmony</c> is a powerful tool for generating and managing <cy>color palettes</c> directly in the level editor, powered by [HueMint.com](https://huemint.com).

Generate palettes of up to <cy>12 colors</c> and preview them in your level in <cg>real time</c>.

---

## Features

### Palette Generation
Generate color palettes with full control over the generation settings.
Supports different <cy>presets</c>, <cy>modes</c>, <cy>creativity</c> levels and custom <cy>adjacency matrices</c> to fine-tune the results to your needs.
> Requires an <cr>internet connection</c>.

### Palette Library
Save your generated palettes for later use. You can give them a <cy>custom name</c> and mark them as <cy>favorites</c> to keep your best discoveries organized.

### Color Harmonies
Explore the color relationships of any color in your palette by clicking its <cy>info button</c>. This opens a popup showing:
- <c-FF6B6B>Complementary</c>, <c-FF7F50>Analogous</c>, <c-FFD93D>Triadic</c>, <c-6BCB77>Tetradic</c>, <c-4D96FF>Split-Complementary</c> and <c-9B72CF>Square</c> harmonies.
- <c-FF6B6B>Shades</c>, <c-FFD93D>Tints</c> and <c-4D96FF>Color Scales</c> derived from the selected color.

### Palette Simulation
Preview how a palette looks in your level <cy>in real time</c>.

Link <cy>palette slots</c> to <cy>color channels</c> in your level through the settings panel. Starting a simulation will <cg>back up</c> all your original colors and replace the linked channels with the colors from the current palette. You can freely experiment with color settings during the simulation.

When you stop, all colors are <cg>fully restored</c> like nothing happened.

## Powered by HueMint

The palette generation in this mod is powered by [HueMint.com](https://huemint.com), a <cy>machine learning</c> platform for generating color palettes for graphic design. This mod uses its <cy>public API</c> to generate palettes based on your settings.

Some advanced settings — especially the <cy>adjacency matrix</c> — may require some background knowledge to get the most out of them. You can learn more about how palette generation works here: [huemint.com/about](https://huemint.com/about/)
